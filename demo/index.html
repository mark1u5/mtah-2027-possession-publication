How v9m1 works

v9m1 combines a non-causal dilated TCN with deterministic physical rules inside a single physically modulated Viterbi decoder — reading only raw ATD tracking.

1 · Learned Branch (TCN v7d)

8 dilated residual blocks (dilation 1→128, receptive field ±20 s) trained on external matches with v3 tracking degradation. Bridges ball dropouts and long gaps.

2 · Physics & Rules (p3_notag)

Closest reachable player + relative ball speed rules without Smart Tagging, paired with invariant contact confidence conf_fisica = exp(-0.20 · v_rel) · 1[κ ≤ 0.95].

3 · Dynamic Modulation

When physical ball control is clear, w_t = w · (1 − factor · conf_t) shifts emission weight from the blurred TCN toward crisp frame-level physical control.

4 · Decoupled Viterbi HMM

Separates minimum spell multipliers for live play (s_P = 1.0, ~1.4 s) and dead ball (s_D = 0.25, ~1.1 s), recovering brief stoppages without fracturing possessions.

External 10-match benchmark: 0.701 Spell-F1 [0.667, 0.735] · 0.893 Macro-F1.
