How the model works

Football tracking data is noisy: automated cameras often lose the ball behind players or during fast kicks. Instead of trusting a single signal, this system combines two views and smooths them into clean possessions.

1 · Context ModelLooks 20 s back & ahead to bridge gaps
2 · Touch PhysicsSpots who is close & controlling the ball
3 · Dynamic BalanceTrusts physics on clear touches, context elsewhere
4 · SmoothingTurns noisy frames into realistic spells

Uses open anonymised clips from Metrica Sports Sample Data · Built with the v9m1 pipeline by Marc Bisbal & Jan Fusté (Team El Chato).
